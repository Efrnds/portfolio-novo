## Purpose notes

- Color: each project page borrows one restrained cue from the real product, while the portfolio chrome remains paper, ink, and orange.
- Layout: composition follows the evidence available for each project. Texsa is text-led because no screenshots exist; Vwrks is image-led because the delivered product is a visual portfolio.
- Typography: Urbanist remains the common authorial voice; scale and alignment change by project rather than switching typefaces decoratively.
- Spacing: wide intervals separate narrative chapters; denser spacing is reserved for metadata and technical evidence.
- Motion: masks reveal real screenshots, parallax gives large images depth, and the route curtain marks a change of case. All motion has a reading or navigation purpose.
- Images: every visual is an existing product screenshot from `public/images`; no decorative assets or fabricated interface were introduced.
