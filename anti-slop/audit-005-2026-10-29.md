Date: 2026-10-29

Status: PASS

## Findings resolved

| ID | Priority | Rule | Finding | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | High | R-01, R-19 | The homepage revealed sections but did not create distinct narrative environments. | Rebuilt the home as five scroll chapters: light introduction, dark operating context, and dedicated Trato, Opinioteca, and Calmera environments with sticky stages and progress-driven motion. |

## Purpose notes

- The pinned sections use the storytelling principle of the referenced Dembélé site without copying its football identity, typography, imagery, or chapter content.
- Each product environment expresses its actual operating context: Trato is a dark control surface, Opinioteca is a cool editorial network, and Calmera is a warm production workspace.
- The copy names real users, workflows, technical responsibilities, and constraints. Technology supports the explanation instead of replacing it.
- The language switch is a compact utility because it changes the meaning of the entire site; its active state is explicit through `aria-pressed`.
- Future product videos can replace a stage screenshot without changing the narrative or layout contract.
