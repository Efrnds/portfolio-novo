# Anti-slop audit 006

Date: 2026-10-29

Scope: home chapter containment, scroll phase timing, project-detail motion, Vwrks and Calmera media layouts

Status: PASS

## Findings resolved

| ID | Priority | Rule | Finding | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Critical | R-03, R-05 | Scale and opacity were applied to the complete home stage, changing the geometry of already constrained project grids and clipping titles and screenshots. | Kept every stage geometrically stable and moved animation to independent heading and body layers. |
| 02 | High | R-19, R-20 | Experience content started moving too early and remained visible when the following light environment entered. | Split the timeline into entry, a long stationary reading phase, full exit, and an empty dark hold before the background changes. |
| 03 | High | R-03, R-24 | Home project compositions exceeded short desktop viewports and relied on oversized heading and card minimum heights. | Removed fixed card minimum heights, reduced sticky-scene type scale, constrained the media body, and added a short-viewport layout adjustment. |
| 04 | High | R-19 | Project-detail motion was limited to one-time reveals and did not carry the scroll-story language into every case. | Added a reusable scroll scene with entry, stable reading, exit, progress, reduced-motion behavior, and mobile fallback; applied it to all five project cases. |
| 05 | High | R-03, R-05 | Vwrks archive images formed an unbounded staggered grid, while Calmera closing images had no viewport contract. | Rebuilt Vwrks as a contained 2×2 media scene and placed Calmera’s closing pair inside a bounded scroll scene. |

## Purpose notes

- The stable interval is intentionally the longest part of every timeline. Motion introduces and dismisses content; it does not compete with reading.
- A blank hold at the end of each environment makes the following background change legible instead of simultaneous with outgoing text.
- Project-detail scenes reuse timing behavior, not visual composition. Each case retains its own grid, colors, typography, and product evidence.
- Mobile and reduced-motion modes remove sticky height and inline transforms, returning every scene to normal document flow.
