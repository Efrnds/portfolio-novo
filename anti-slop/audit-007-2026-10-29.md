# Anti-slop audit 007

Date: 2026-10-29

Scope: opacity rollback and scroll-controlled home media carousel

Status: PASS

## Findings resolved

| ID | Priority | Rule | Finding | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Critical | R-19, R-20 | Opacity animation was applied beyond the requested Experience transition and weakened imagery across the site. | Removed animated opacity from the hero, work heading, project cards, project cases, Opinioteca view switcher, screenshot carousel, and footer. Experience is the only narrative section that fades. |
| 02 | Critical | R-03, R-05 | Multiple home screenshots occupied separate grid regions and exceeded the available sticky-stage height. | Replaced each project media collage with one bounded viewport containing exactly two full-size slides. |
| 03 | High | R-19, R-26 | Image changes were disconnected from the home scroll narrative. | Bound both slides to project scroll progress: the first translates upward while the second enters from below in the same frame, without crossfade. |
| 04 | High | R-06, R-24 | An absolutely layered carousel could collapse without an explicit mobile height. | Added definite mobile media heights for Trato, Opinioteca, and Calmera while preserving normal page flow. |

## Purpose notes

- Opacity remains in Experience because its complete disappearance is the explicit transition signal before the background changes.
- Everywhere else, motion uses translation, clipping, masking, or direct slide replacement. Product screenshots remain fully opaque.
- The two-image viewport prevents media from changing card geometry. The carousel changes evidence, not layout.
- The swap occurs during the stable middle of each sticky chapter, leaving time to inspect both states.
