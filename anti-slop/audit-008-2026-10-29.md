# Anti-slop audit 008

Date: 2026-10-29

Scope: scroll smoothing, Calmera chapter exit, archive boundary, full-screen footer

Status: PASS

## Findings resolved

| ID | Priority | Rule | Finding | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Critical | R-19, R-20 | Scroll transforms consumed the raw wheel position and therefore reacted in visible steps. | Routed hero, Experience, home cases, media swaps, progress rails, and internal case scenes through tuned spring motion values. |
| 02 | Critical | R-03, R-20 | The Calmera scene translated only 130 px at exit and remained partially visible when the archive entered. | Extended the exit beyond one viewport height, moved it earlier in the timeline, and reserved the final chapter interval as an empty hold. |

| 04 | High | R-01, R-20 | The footer read as another content strip instead of the terminal state of the portfolio. | Made the footer a full-viewport final environment with a vertically centered CTA and metadata anchored at the bottom. |

## Purpose notes

- Spring damping removes wheel-step jitter without introducing autoplay or detached animation.
- Calmera’s content exits through translation only; no opacity was reintroduced.
- The archive begins only after the product content has cleared the viewport, making the background change an explicit chapter boundary.
- The full-screen footer functions as a clear narrative stop and keeps the primary contact action visible without an overlay.
